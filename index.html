About Us

Welcome to iDecor – where creativity meets celebration!

What began as a hobby in 2016 has now blossomed into a passionate journey of bringing joy, color, and elegance to every event we touch. From small family gatherings to grand stage decorations, we pour our heart into every setup, ensuring it reflects the vision and emotions of our clients.

Event decoration has always been more than just a service to us – it’s a true passion. We love transforming ordinary spaces into beautiful, unforgettable experiences that leave lasting impressions. Over the years, we’ve had the pleasure of working with amazing people and turning their special occasions into picture-perfect moments.

Whether you’re planning a wedding, birthday, cultural event, or corporate function, iDecor is here to add that personal, magical touch. We believe every event deserves to be unique, memorable, and stress-free – and We're here to make that happen for you.

Thank you for trusting us with your celebrations. We can’t wait to be a part of your story!
